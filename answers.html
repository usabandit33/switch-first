Common questions

Is a transfer switch the same as a whole-home standby package?

No. A switch or interlock is hardware at the panel. A package usually adds the generator, pad, fuel line, electrical work, and often the permit. Ask which lines are on the paper.

Can I install a standby generator myself in Houston?

Permanent standby work typically involves licensed electrical and gas trades plus a city permit and inspection. Ask whose license and permit name are on the job. This site is not legal advice.

Do I need an automatic transfer switch or will an interlock work?

They are different products. An interlock or manual switch is not the same as an automatic transfer switch that starts a standby unit. Ask which hardware and which load rating are in the bid.

Who pulls the City of Houston electrical permit?

Ask whether a City of Houston electrical permit is included and whose name is on it before you sign.

Does the quote include gas line and pad?

Many Houston quotes split the box, the switch, the pad, and the gas run. Ask which are included.
